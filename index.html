01 About

I'm a Computer Engineering student in Drexel University's College of Engineering. My work lives where silicon meets code: optimizing computation on reconfigurable hardware, driving sensors and microcontrollers close to the metal, and exploring what quantum and ML can do for hard problems.

Recent work includes research on optimized computing for FPGAs (comparing RTL against HLS), a senior-year quantum computing project, applied ML/AI coursework, and a collection of embedded drivers for ESP32 and Raspberry Pi platforms.
