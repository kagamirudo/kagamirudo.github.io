01 About

I'm a Master's student in Computer Engineering at Drexel University's College of Engineering (Pennoni Honors College), concentrating in computer systems & architecture and algorithms & data structures. I completed my B.S. in Computer Science at Drexel in 2025 with minors in Computer Engineering and Mathematics.

My research sits where silicon meets theory. Right now I'm building a compiler for FPGA nested-loop acceleration — turning dense linear-algebra kernels into high-utilization systolic arrays. Earlier I designed a quantum algorithm for lexicographically-minimum string rotation and ran it on IBM Quantum hardware, and most recently I've been writing a short series of combinatorics notes on adjacency-free colorings of the Rubik's cube.

I'm applying to PhD programs to keep working at this intersection. Alongside research I build production embedded systems — edge CV, DAQ pipelines, and firmware — which keeps my theory grounded in real hardware constraints.
